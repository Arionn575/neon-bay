Neon Bay — an original sun-soaked coastal open-world crime game. Vic Marlowe, fresh out of prison, works for Rosa Delgado at the Palms Diner and settles the score with Eddie Salazar.

On foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, stealing cars, a wanted system and police chases, a 4-mission story chain, pistol combat, a HUD with a minimap, start and save, and sunset polish. Everything original — no Rockstar name, logo, character, map, or asset.
